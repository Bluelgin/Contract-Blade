# Oath Manifestation pixel artwork

The approved Oath Ring design is **B — Twin Contract / 双契之戒**:
a silver ring with paired cyan and rose crystals. The center remains transparent.
The two crystals represent the owner and maid fighting together.

- Item: `oath_ring.svg` → 32×32 RGBA `textures/item/oath_ring.png`.
- Effect: `oath_backlash.svg` → 18×18 RGBA `textures/mob_effect/oath_backlash.png`.
- Rebuild both using `python art/oath/export.py` (Inkscape required).

The ring geometry is original pixel SVG. Botania and The Aether item sprites were
studied for readable hollow silhouettes, compact settings and material highlights;
no third-party bitmap is included or traced into the final asset.
