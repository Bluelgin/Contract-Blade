# Standing iaido

Separate first-phase executor and synchronized client presentation; `STANDING_IAIDO` is appended to the skill enum so existing ordinals remain unchanged.

Timeline: track target during the 2.5-second charge until tick 46, lock direction, draw at 48, single arena-wide hit at 50, recovery ends at 70. Damage is 60, scoped to the encounter challenger; stepping sideways or reaching a distant arena corner does not evade it. A fresh parry cancels damage without requiring the player to face the boss, protects the player for 24 ticks and staggers the boss. An existing successful-parry protection window also prevents this hit. The boss steps forward at most 0.8 blocks if collision checks permit. Ordinary hits still deal damage but cannot break this charge through posture.

The weighted skill pool adds one seat with an eight-second entry cooldown. It excludes consecutive standing/dimensional grand slashes. The phase-two final stand is documented in `BLACK_FOX_FINAL_STAND.md`.

Animation/equipment reuse the approved iaido clip and sheath-mouth locator, with a timeline adapter stretching only the charge. Rendering samples up to 24 native `ss.obj` cosmetic swords from the shared model manager. Staggered seats repeatedly emit inward-moving swords every twelve ticks until the last complete wave before aim lock; near the body they shrink into light and disappear. Existing rifts and sheath-mouth charge artwork are reused. No projectile entity, collision, damage, packets or persistent effect tracker is created for these gathering swords. Cancellation hides everything through the synchronized skill state.

The attack uses a single server damage/parry decision, not the player's complete right-click ability. `BlackFoxArenaSlashVisual` now renders an expanding circular slash reaching the farthest arena corner from its fixed hit snapshot. Four existing edge/face/halo/remnant textures are reused over 128 segments, at most 2048 vertices per frame, with a 24-tick lifetime. Damage is not repeated as the visual expands. The smaller curved arc remains available to ordinary/dimensional cuts and the standalone artwork preview.

The arc bends four authored SVG-derived textures over a shared 64-segment path: narrow cutting edge, broad opaque purple face with fractured color planes, faint blurred halo and broad torn remnants. Each layer has its own width, opacity and point-local lifetime (8/16/16/20 ticks after the sweep reaches that point). The face holds until eight local ticks, then disappears in staggered chunks; remnants appear later and drift inward. Face/remnants use alpha blending, while edge/halo are additive, all with the existing vanilla emissive shader, fullbright, depth testing and no depth writes. Assets are in `art/black_fox/arc_slash`; rebuild with `tools/build_black_fox_arc.cjs` and the bundled Sharp dependency. No effect entities or per-layer gameplay timers.

The inner edge also bends three sections of the existing rift artwork and energy mask along the same arc. Each section opens behind the advancing slash and closes inward as the face tears away. `BlackFoxArcRiftVisual` owns this presentation separately and uses the existing energy shader/fallback, with point-local clocks and no new entities, textures, packets or global shader uniforms. The solid arc layers now use the vanilla unlit eyes shader instead of the directionally shaded entity-emissive shader, keeping horizontal cuts bright without removing their alpha-blended color planes.

Dimensional large-slash world placement is at 0.9 blocks above the boss base, pitched 75 degrees into a near-horizontal cut instead of a tall upright arch. The main face is brighter, retaining the initial four-tick sweep and up to 24 ticks of hold/remnants. Standing iaido instead uses the arena-wide ring at roughly one block above its hit snapshot. Ordinary profiles use the accelerating sector described below. Native Combo B effects and ordinary damage/parry windows remain unchanged.

Ordinary cut profiles now use a separate sector sweep rather than scaling the large arc. Server ordinary cuts and presentation share `MELEE_REACH=6` and `MELEE_DOT=0.8` (about 74 degrees total). The solid blade and rift vertices remain inside that horizontal reach/cone; server distance continues to be the existing 3D distance check. A ten-tick accelerating sweep uses point arrival `10*sqrt(along)`. Local core/face/halo/remnant lifetimes are 2.5/3.5/4/6 ticks: earlier sections disappear as later ones arrive, with a sixteen-tick total window. Large charged slashes retain their heavier hold. The old zero-damage native slash overlay is suppressed for these ordinary profiles so it cannot visually contradict the new range or progression; native Combo B is unchanged. This is a visual follow-through of the existing single server hit, not a moving damage entity or a changed parry window.

Actual server cut execution records one snapshot: arena-local origin, yaw, roll, skill kind and world birth tick. Arena origin is synchronized once at initialization to retain precision in distant cells. `BlackFoxSlashEffects` renders that snapshot independently of later skill changes and cancels the exact `xOld/yOld/zOld` interpolated entity transform used by LevelRenderer. It never follows a later blink and does not use the current skill age to infer a hit. Large client position jumps snap rather than interpolate across the arena. Parrying does not erase an already executed cut; interrupted windup produces no snapshot. At most one snapshot is retained, with no effect entity, per-frame packet or growing tracker.

Native server validation checks charge safety, arena-corner damage, parry/protection, interrupted cleanup and snapshot stability across teleportation. Native client validation renders 61 cosmetic-sword/charge/preview frames, 26 actual arena-ring frames and 57 arc frames, checks five melee cut windows and world height, and verifies all four texture resources. Public stable release is unchanged.
